## Boss v20.2 — Full-game save recovery (released 2026-10-17)

**Why this release exists:** A long-running iPhone Safari company repeatedly returned to Day 2642 on refresh. The entire company's progress, not just editable settings, was failing to persist. The exact on-device browser failure code cannot be inferred remotely.

**Recovery design:** Each successful browser save is verified and shows the committed game day. If regular `localStorage` rejects a save, Boss pauses progression while making a transactional, offline `IndexedDB` save. It resumes only after that transaction is committed. On reload Boss compares verified revisions and restores the newer on-device company; old `boss19` / `boss20` local data is **not cleared, overwritten to free space, or fabricated**. Browser conflicts or failure in both stores block further simulation rather than pretending progress has saved. A top-of-screen warning describes the error and provides **Export game backup**, which prepares a JSON file from the current in-memory company. Do not use Restart or clear Safari website data to fix a save error.

**Verified release:** [PR #21](https://github.com/BSAVCI1/Boss/pull/21) merged at production commit `6f227439f0639a960e6ccd2481ad890c9ed27509`. [Production regressions](https://github.com/BSAVCI1/Boss/actions/runs/39060489413) and [GitHub Pages](https://github.com/BSAVCI1/Boss/actions/runs/39060489423) succeeded. Real 390px Chromium tests verified injected localStorage failure → transactional IndexedDB commit → refresh restoring newer game day → one business-line purchase with exactly one $120 investment, with no console errors. This is not a direct test of the owner's private Safari data. An exported backup file can be reviewed for a device-specific diagnosis without modifying the browser save.

## How to play
**Today — CEO office:** See what the company sells, its current chapter and next challenge, your direct-report structure, the optional Executive Assistant and the executive inbox. Select a **90-day company direction** and tap **Set CEO direction** to confirm it. The selection stays pending rather than changing policy silently during simulation ticks. Directors prepare weekly, costed recommendations; you approve or decline the major decisions at executive meetings. Outcomes, including recurring wage impacts, are recorded in the decision history.

**Work:** Review customer contracts, opportunities, delivery and pipeline. Managers handle in-department assignments and employee development within explicit CEO authority; manually assigned work remains protected. Use the separate Team → Leadership hierarchy controls to define up to four optional team managers. Open **Business records & money** only when you want financial reports, credit, invoices or payroll detail.

**Team:** Appoint Commercial and Delivery department heads, then optionally appoint Sales, Marketing, Product and Operations team managers when reporting spans grow. Expand reporting lines only when needed. Distinct staff IDs clarify repeated names. A department head's actual leadership career level determines whether the CEO office calls them Department Manager or Director; appointments and career ranks remain separate. The Executive Assistant coordinates CEO follow-ups and cross-team exceptions, without replacing departmental authority.

**Growth:** Explore customer industries separately from services sold, competition, market briefs, experiments and company knowledge. Optional service lines cost **$120 in game money** each, share your actual staff, and start their own revenue attribution only on subsequent business. Completed capabilities are archived, while R&D remains available.

The game works offline in one page with no login or real purchases. Choose 1×, 3× or 8× speed, Pause, or optional advance of up to seven game days. Focus pauses new work intake, not salaries or existing deadlines. **Browser progress is saved automatically on the same device/browser.** Restart is under Today → Game settings; it clears the current game after confirmation.
