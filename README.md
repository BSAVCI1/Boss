# Boss

Boss is a free, ad-free, offline-capable, mobile-first, endless leader simulation. Begin with a three-person team, then recruit specialists, support people, and lead through client work, learning, experimentation, dilemmas, and company decisions. No sign-in, tracking, external libraries, fonts or network requests are required.

**Play:** https://bsavci1.github.io/Boss/.

## How to play
Tap a person’s name on a client deal to assign or unassign them. Use Study, Teach, Experiment or Rest to guide the team. Under **Recruitment & team culture**, filter candidates by skill and personal interest, search for four candidates, and hire those you can afford. Each hire costs a one-time fee and adds $1.50/day in salaries. Interests influence performance and learning. Every person has **Recognize** (free), **Bonus** ($12), and **Coach** ($8) actions with a seven-day cooldown, plus a **Fire** button (the last employee cannot be dismissed). Team activities cost $5 per person and have a 12-day cooldown. Respond to dilemmas and combine discovered ideas into lasting breakthroughs. Choose 1x, 3x or 8x speed, Pause, or Restart. **Focus mode** suspends new client inquiries and new dilemmas without stopping progress on existing work (deadlines and salaries still apply). By default, new clients arrive about every 12–20 game days and never less than 25 real seconds apart, while dilemmas are separated by at least 45 seconds of real play. Appoint an existing employee as an **office assistant**, or hire one for $20; the assistant follows your customizable delegation rules. Open **Delegation rules** to choose automatic assignment (on/off), prioritization (balanced, deadline-first, or skills-first), an automatic spending cap, and individual permissions for praise, family leave, coaching mistakes, or supporting experiments. Raises, conflicts, departures, and rival offers remain your decisions. The **Project priorities & deadline forecast** shows days remaining, projected work time, worker status, and risk even without an assistant. Deadline warnings are logged once per risk change. Your direct orders always take priority. Progress saves automatically in the browser.

## How to change the game
All the HTML, CSS and JavaScript live in one file, [index.html](index.html). Edit this complete file and commit it to `main`; GitHub Pages automatically publishes the root of `main` shortly afterward. The browser save key is `boss8` (previous `boss7`, `boss6`, `boss5`, and `tl3` saves are imported automatically; open legacy projects are shortened and repriced once)., so preserve save compatibility. Also record each change in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and the Boss Notion page.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages is enabled with **Deploy from a branch → main → /(root)**. Pushing changes to `main` automatically rebuilds and publishes the site. View [deployment runs](https://github.com/BSAVCI1/Boss/actions) for status.

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for planned features; new roadmap features are proposals, not yet implemented.

## Current: Boss v8 — Projects & Customers
- **Shorter, better-paid opening contracts:** typically 8–9 units of work with initial base fees around $80–$90 (higher with sector focus), before reputation effects. A three-person starting team still costs $4.50 per game day. Progress carries into a staged Plan → Build → Review process; project quality affects customer loyalty.
- **Project negotiation:** one-time premium scope (+25% fee, slightly more work), quick delivery (less scope, -8% fee and quality compromise), or client-first delivery (more time, higher quality, -5% fee). Any in-progress project can request one deadline extension (+8 days, -10% fee, -1 reputation) and one $8 quality review.
- **Customer relationships:** every successful or late project changes customer trust; repeat customers can return and are tracked in the **Customer relationships** portfolio with loyalty, delivered quality and completed contract count.
- **Sector strategy and expansion:** start in Hospitality and Retail, select a focus for +12% payout and speed; unlock Creative ($55, reputation 6; +12% sector premium), Logistics ($75, rep 12; +20%), and Technology ($95, rep 20; +30%). All investments are in-game only and permanent within that playthrough.
- **Evolving complexity:** stages 1–5 increase as the company completes contracts (a new stage about every five completions); later sectors and advanced contracts pay more. When there are no projects, the next game-day-qualified lead can arrive without waiting for the 25-second real-time cooldown, preventing high-speed insolvency. Focus mode still blocks new intake.
