# Boss

Boss is a free, ad-free, offline-capable, mobile-first, endless leader simulation. Begin with a three-person team, then recruit specialists, support people, and lead through client work, learning, experimentation, dilemmas, and company decisions. No sign-in, tracking, external libraries, fonts or network requests are required.

**Play:** https://bsavci1.github.io/Boss/.

## How to play
Tap a person’s name on a client deal to assign or unassign them. Use Study, Teach, Experiment or Rest to guide the team. Under **Recruitment & team culture**, filter candidates by skill and personal interest, search for four candidates, and hire those you can afford. Each hire costs a one-time fee and adds $1.50/day in salaries. Interests influence performance and learning. Every person has **Recognize** (free), **Bonus** ($12), and **Coach** ($8) actions with a seven-day cooldown, plus a **Fire** button (the last employee cannot be dismissed). Team activities cost $5 per person and have a 12-day cooldown. Respond to meaningful dilemmas; connected ideas now unlock capabilities automatically, without manual matching. Choose 1x, 3x or 8x speed using the compact top selector, or Pause. Restart is under Today → Office animation & game settings. **Focus mode** suspends new client inquiries and new dilemmas without stopping progress on existing work (deadlines and salaries still apply). In v10, employees build a visible sales pipeline rather than waiting for randomly generated contracts. Accepted contracts are at least eight game days apart; dilemmas are separated by at least 45 seconds of real play. Appoint an existing employee as an **office assistant**, or hire one for $20; the assistant follows your customizable delegation rules. Open **Delegation rules** to choose automatic assignment (on/off), prioritization (balanced, deadline-first, or skills-first), an automatic spending cap, and individual permissions for praise, family leave, coaching mistakes, or supporting experiments. Raises, conflicts, departures, and rival offers remain your decisions. The **Project priorities & deadline forecast** shows days remaining, projected work time, worker status, and risk even without an assistant. Deadline warnings are logged once per risk change. Your direct orders always take priority. Progress saves automatically in the browser.

## How to change the game
All the HTML, CSS and JavaScript live in one file, [index.html](index.html). Edit this complete file and commit it to `main`; GitHub Pages automatically publishes the root of `main` shortly afterward. The browser save key is `boss15` (automatically migrates `boss12`, `boss10` and earlier supported save versions). Existing `boss9`, `boss8`, `boss7`, `boss6`, `boss5`, and `tl3` progress is imported automatically. Existing project balances remain unchanged for v8 and v9 saves. Earlier projects retain the existing compatibility migration. Also record each change in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and the Boss Notion page.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages is enabled with **Deploy from a branch → main → /(root)**. Pushing changes to `main` automatically rebuilds and publishes the site. View [deployment runs](https://github.com/BSAVCI1/Boss/actions) for status.

## Previous: Boss v8 — Projects & Customers
- **Shorter, better-paid opening contracts:** typically 8–9 units of work with initial base fees around $80–$90 (higher with sector focus), before reputation effects. A three-person starting team still costs $4.50 per game day. Progress carries into a staged Plan → Build → Review process; project quality affects customer loyalty.
- **Project negotiation:** one-time premium scope (+25% fee, slightly more work), quick delivery (less scope, -8% fee and quality compromise), or client-first delivery (more time, higher quality, -5% fee). Any in-progress project can request one deadline extension (+8 days, -10% fee, -1 reputation) and one $8 quality review.
- **Customer relationships:** every successful or late project changes customer trust; repeat customers can return and are tracked in the **Customer relationships** portfolio with loyalty, delivered quality and completed contract count.
- **Sector strategy and expansion:** start in Hospitality and Retail, select a focus for +12% payout and speed; unlock Creative ($55, reputation 6; +12% sector premium), Logistics ($75, rep 12; +20%), and Technology ($95, rep 20; +30%). All investments are in-game only and permanent within that playthrough.
- **Evolving complexity:** stages 1–5 increase as the company completes contracts (a new stage about every five completions); later sectors and advanced contracts pay more. When there are no projects, the next game-day-qualified lead can arrive without waiting for the 25-second real-time cooldown, preventing high-speed insolvency. Focus mode still blocks new intake.

## Boss v9 — Smarter Employees (delivered)
- **Individual goals:** each employee cares about growth, recognition, serving clients, healthy balance, stability, or creative freedom. Goals influence satisfaction and, for aligned work, productivity.
- **Workload and burnout:** stress accumulates from projects, tight deadlines and experimentation; breaks, check-ins, team activities and coaching provide relief. Persistent burnout reduces speed and quality and takes time to recover. Staff under severe pressure may take a four-day recovery pause without opening another dilemma.
- **Hidden talents:** each employee has a stable, initially unknown skill affinity with a visible clue. Observation through study, experimentation, teaching, relevant work or coaching can reveal it and deliver a one-time skill bonus.
- **Career decisions:** open a colleague's **Career & wellbeing** panel for stress, burnout, satisfaction, XP, milestones and hidden talent clues. **Check-in** is free (9-day cooldown), **Growth plan** costs $10 (14-day cooldown), and **Promote** costs $25 once experience and skill thresholds are met. Senior and Lead roles improve performance but add $0.75/day to payroll per rank.
- **Autonomy:** overwhelmed workers can recover without interrupting you; assistant delegation does not override a recovery pause. Player task orders still take priority.
- **Keep existing features:** project economics, sectors and contracts, recruiting, smart assistant, Focus mode, dilemmas and discoveries all remain available. The experience is offline and stored locally.

## Boss v10 — Company Operating System (delivered)
- **Continuous commercial pipeline:** prospects move through qualification, proposals and negotiation before a project is won. Sales employees prospect, Marketing generates demand, and organic inquiries supplement the pipeline. A won project is scheduled when delivery capacity is available, not thrown at the player as an immediate interruption. Contracts have an eight-game-day minimum spacing. Focus mode blocks new project starts but does not erase leads or stop them maturing.
- **Workforce roles:** each person owns Sales, Marketing, Product, Operations, or (after earning enough seniority/experience) Team Lead responsibilities. Opening staff default to Sales/Product/Operations. Change a primary role from the employee card. Role-based work builds skills and career experience.
- **Autonomous work plans:** global operating policies are Balanced, Growth, and Innovation; an adjustable target sets qualified pipeline coverage. Toggle automatic idle task planning globally or switch a specific employee to manual instructions until you resume their autonomous plan. Team members prospect, market, study, teach, experiment, or rest based on roles, backlog, personal goals, and stress. Manual orders remain protected. Dedicated office assistants, or qualifying team leads in their absence, can reassign staff from autonomous tasks to urgent paid work.
- **Company knowledge/capability map:** employee discoveries automatically fill a visual dependency map, with clear 0/2, 1/2, or active capability states. No trial-and-error pair selection: the previous breakthroughs and modifiers automatically unlock when both prerequisites exist, once only. Original discoveries and built capabilities migrate from prior saves.
- **Employee skill map:** team-level skills, strongest mentor suggestions, each person's main role and discovered or undiscovered talent.
- **Unchanged:** paid client projects and negotiation, milestones, quality, customer loyalty, sector focus and expansion, hiring/firing, career development, smart delegation spending caps, Focus mode, office simulation, and the offline single-file design.
- **QA:** functional scenarios include full pipeline progression, passive leads, autonomous study/marketing/sales, manual-command protection, leader delegation, capability migration and single application, uninterrupted Focus, and long-run financial behavior.

## Current: Boss v12 — Business Intelligence (delivered)
- **Executive KPIs:** Today shows earned revenue, operating costs, operating profit and operating margin, without crowding the v11 executive briefing.
- **Work → Business intelligence:** expand the performance panel or tap **View full performance** from Today. Select the last 30 days, 90 days or all measured history. Reports contain financial P&L, received and paid cash, payroll liability, income/cost trends, expense categories, investments, lead quality by source, estimated sales acquisition labor cost, delivery value/quality/on-time delivery, project-level contribution, sector performance and employee utilization.
- **Accounting semantics:** booked contract value records an estimated award fee at the time of signing and updates with the contract's negotiated changes. Earned revenue is recorded on successful completion, with payment received immediately under the existing Boss rules. The game does **not** yet simulate invoicing, receivables or delayed collection; those belong to the later advanced economy release. Operating profit is earned revenue minus payroll due and operating expenses. Cash flow reflects actual receipts and payments. Unpaid payroll is flagged as a liability; new-sector investments consume cash but are not expensed in operating profit (depreciation is not modeled).
- **Delivery economics:** direct labor is allocated by the worker's daily salary while producing a customer project, with quality-review costs assigned to that project. Project contribution is fee received minus directly allocated labor and project costs; company overhead is reflected in operating profit, not counted twice.
- **Commercial metrics:** pipeline tracks lead creation, qualification, proposals, negotiations, wins, lost leads and expiry by source. Conversion is based on observed events, **not** a perfect lead-cohort calculation; the estimated acquisition cost uses the salary expense of employees working on sales/marketing divided by deals won in the selected period. No advertising spend is simulated.
- **Historical integrity:** for an existing game, the reporting baseline starts at the game day on which Boss v12 first loads that save. Old cash and all gameplay progress are preserved, but historical financial data is **not fabricated**. Old active contracts are identified as untracked bookings and generate real revenue entries only upon their future delivery. Reports keep lifetime cumulative totals and the most recent 366 game days of day-by-day trends, plus compact transaction/project history.
- **Save and deployment:** storage key `boss12`; migrates `boss10`, `boss9` and earlier supported formats. Still one standalone offline HTML file without network dependencies or analytics. No changes to core contract, hiring, delegation or salary behaviors.
- **Tests:** KPI rendering, booking vs. earned revenue, expenses, cost allocation, lead transitions, 30/90/all views, cash reconciliation, profitable long-run gameplay, legacy-save baseline and 450-day economy equivalence against v11.

## Current: Boss v13 — Organization & Leadership (delivered)
- **Real reporting lines:** the **Team → Organization & management** section distinguishes **Commercial (Sales / Marketing)** and **Delivery (Product / Operations)**. Every employee belongs to a department, can be reassigned in their employee card, and keeps their own specialization, goals and career history. Selecting another specialist role automatically follows the corresponding department; Team Lead is a role but has no management authority until appointed to a specific department.
- **Manager appointment:** choose **Department budgets, authority & targets**, then select a manager who has Senior career rank or at least 10 experience points. The appointed manager leads only direct reports in their department; managers cannot automatically assign work or coach in unrelated departments. The founder remains responsible when a department has no manager. The office assistant retains whole-company coordination.
- **Management costs and benefits:** managers receive **+$0.75 salary/day** while appointed, stop routine billable individual delivery, and can improve their direct reports' work speed by up to **7%** when actively teaching/coaching; large reporting spans reduce effectiveness. Every seven game days, an on-duty manager may spend $4 to coach a selected direct report, provided funds and the department's coaching budget allow it. Coaching increases relevant skills and satisfaction and reduces stress. This is an operational tradeoff, not a free productivity bonus.
- **Delegation authority:** the office assistant can route cross-department work. Without an assistant, each appointed on-duty manager may assign only projects belonging to their own department, to eligible autonomous direct reports. Your explicit manual orders remain protected. Manager reassignment, dismissal, changes of specialty, or appointment as office assistant revoke conflicting manager authority.
- **Accountable 30-day scorecards:** Commercial tracks qualified lead generation and won business against a selectable lead-volume target (3, 5 or 8); Delivery tracks on-time completion including missed deadlines against a 70%, 85% or 95% target. View reporting lines, direct-report count, coaching spending and target status on compact cards. Budget settings: $0, $8, $16 or $30 per 30 game days. Stats begin when v13 first opens an older save, rather than fabricating prior departmental performance.
- **Progression:** you do not need several managers to run a three-person company; remain flat initially, then appoint managers when company size and leadership readiness justify costs. Department scorecards complement the Finance / Sales / Delivery / People KPI dashboard.
- **Guardrails and validation:** standalone mobile-first offline `index.html`; local `boss13` save automatically imports earlier versions, including all v12 financial history. Side-by-side 360-day regression with no appointed managers retained identical cash, delivery outcomes and financial KPIs, alongside 27 functional management checks.

**Manager reviews and handovers:** Every 30 game days, the department records whether it met its objective, the metric used, its manager, coaching expense and budget. The previous review remains visible when the next 30-day period begins. Stepping down, changing departments or replacing a manager restores specialist responsibilities and salary instead of trapping a person in a non-delivery role. An unappointed Team Lead may still support delivery as an autonomous specialist.

## Boss v14 — Entrepreneurship & Leadership Lab (delivered)
- **Growth → Entrepreneurship & Leadership Lab:** optional, collapsible learning notebook with six business experiments: grow demand, improve delivery reliability, develop staff skills, protect wellbeing, specialize in a sector, or test a custom leadership decision.
- **Hypothesis → intervention → outcome → reflection:** before beginning, choose a 15/30/45-day horizon, predicted percentage change (from 25% lower to 50% higher), and an optional hypothesis. Starting an experiment applies the named policy changes exactly once, without additional payments or mandatory interruptions. Only one experiment can run at a time. Direct player decisions remain permitted.
- **Measurable outcomes:** snapshot all business intelligence counters and starting cash, policies, skill levels and stress. Baselines use the 30 preceding tracked days for flow metrics or the starting team for skills/wellbeing, and explicitly show when historical data or completed projects are insufficient. The scorecard compares predicted vs. actual results for the main metric, with revenue, operating costs, profit, leads, sales wins, deliveries, on-time rate, employee utilization and cash change.
- **Honest learning:** simulated results reflect other changing factors (random inquiries, staffing, work backlogs, decisions). A single experiment does not prove real-world cause and effect. If a baseline is missing or near zero, the forecast is marked inconclusive; the player can still review actual outcomes. If an experimental policy is changed again during the run, the scorecard notes that confounder. Interventions remain in effect after the experiment until the player changes them; no hidden rollback.
- **Notebook:** up to 12 completed experiments and editable reflections are saved locally in the browser. Active experiments survive reload and complete on the proper game day without popups. Previous project/customer/people systems and all v13 finance history are preserved. Offline self-contained HTML, no external calls or account needed.
- **Compatibility and tests:** save key `boss14` imports `boss13` and all previously compatible saves. At least seven tracked days are needed for historical comparisons (three completed projects for on-time rates); starting team averages are available immediately. Core-game economic equivalence is checked with no experiment active.

## Boss v15 — Advanced Business Economy and Talent Strategy
- **Work → Advanced economy** (collapsed until needed) separates signed contract fees, earned revenue, customer invoices, receivables, and actual collections. New game starter projects still pay immediately; future agreements use the selected payment term (0, 7, 14 or 30 game days). Bills collect automatically on their due day, with a bounded three-day lateness for some simulated invoices. Early collection is optional at a transparent 3% financing cost.
- **Contract trade-offs:** immediate terms reduce new contract fees 4%; 14 days offer +4% and 30 days +8%, but delay cash. Standard / Competitive (-6%) / Premium (+10%) new-contract pricing affects win probability. Seasonal demand/competition shift across transparent 45-day cycles; existing agreed prices remain locked.
- **Working capital:** optional $50 credit draws up to $150 principal, interest accruing at 0.1% of principal per game day, and voluntary repayments up to $50. Cash receipts from borrowing are *not sales*, principal repayments are *not operating expenses*, and interest expense accrues once. Payroll arrears and invoices remain separate liabilities/assets. The balance sheet is simplified and models no tax or depreciation.
- **Capital investment:** optional Delivery toolkit ($65, +6% employee delivery speed) or Sales toolkit ($55, +8% commercial prospecting). One-time purchases use capital cash, not operating profit. The economy panel displays measured company contribution and wins since each investment, explicitly not pretending these are attributable incremental ROI.
- **Careers and skills:** Associate → Senior retains 8 XP but requires Lv 1.85 in the *actual role*; Senior → Lead needs 24 XP and Lv 2.60 in the actual role, rather than allowing any unrelated skill. Both retain 18-day cooldown, $25 immediate cost and +$0.75/day payroll. Team → employee → Career & wellbeing now allows focused study and growth-plan investment in Sales, Marketing, Product or Operations. Team → Employee skills shows a collapsible skill coverage map, advanced specialist backups, project demand, suggested trainees and quick access to matching candidate searches. Existing ranks, XP, history and pay are retained.
- **Migration:** save key `boss15` imports `boss141`, `boss14` and earlier supported saves. Already signed contracts keep their old payment timing and fee; no pre-upgrade invoices, employee skills, or revenues are fabricated. The previous local save is not deleted. This is one standalone offline HTML file with no external services or data tracking.
