## Delegated hiring

Manager, Senior Manager and Director appointments are actual corporate leadership roles. Every seven days, eligible managers assess real unassigned projects and whether idle colleagues can cover that workload. When there is a true capacity gap, they propose a Sales/Marketing or Product/Operations hire within their corporate functional area, subject to a 30-day request cooldown. Requests appear on Today, in Executive Assistant weekly reports, and in Team → Leadership journey → Corporate staffing decisions.

The CEO can approve or decline an individual recruitment request or set explicit 90-day budget and maximum hire slots. The default is zero authority, including migrated saves. Authorized hires cost $20 each up front and incur normal recurring payroll. Before hiring, cash must cover the cost and 21 days of total projected payroll. An expired, fully spent or exhausted delegation leaves the request for CEO review, without hiring or charging automatically.

## GM access to corporate specialists

Growth → Operating units → Temporary corporate support allows the CEO to authorize an available corporate specialist for 30 days, exclusive to one operating unit while active. The person keeps their corporate reporting line, unique ID and recurring wages. A unit GM can assign eligible future unit contracts only within the authorization period. The allocation itself has no fee. Unit financial reports attribute precisely the existing payroll incurred for actual corporate worker-days on the unit job, without posting additional corporate payroll or treating dedicated-team labor as shared cost. Mixed-worker contracts and expirations are regression tested.

## Safety and scope boundaries

All mutations keep the existing fail-closed verified browser-save path and forward-only migration. Historical revenues, contracts, paid services, salaries and employee histories are not repriced. Editable fields stage changes until explicitly applied; native selectors remain protected from live game redraws. Automated tests cover legacy saves, fresh companies, delegated and CEO-recruited staff, duplicate-action prevention, budget expiry and spare-capacity restraint, 390/414px mobile Chromium, IndexedDB fallback and 730-day cash reconciliation.

**Not implemented in this release:** autonomous performance/salary planning for every subordinate management tier, completed company stage unlock rules, property, investments, acquisitions, warehouses or factories. v21.5 will implement safe service/new-business progression gates.
