Reporting linesFlat founder-led structure
Individual management controls · subject to CEO policies

CEO authorizes one shared budget per department, not a budget per individual manager. Appointed team managers can use that department's coaching envelope for their own direct reports. Hiring slots and the extra payroll cap also apply to the entire department—not to a manager's reporting span. No maximum team size is set here; a wide span reduces management effectiveness.

Appointment and career grade are separate. Department heads earn an additional $0.75/day and team managers $0.50/day, with reduced individual delivery capacity. These appointments do not automatically promote career grade. Team managers coach their team; autonomous hiring, training and department-wide wellbeing remain department-head decisions. The Executive Assistant coordinates CEO follow-ups, not departmental spending.

Business lines

Purchased service lines start as shared service portfolios. You can now organize a purchased line into an operating unit below, appoint a General Manager and allocate dedicated employees. These units are internal operating divisions, not independent subsidiaries or separate bank accounts. Company cash and financial records remain consolidated; unit results only track future work. Shared department budgets and advanced unit delegations evolve in later releases. Revenue is never counted twice.

Launch a new business line

New launches require proven delivery, leadership, qualified staffing and financial runway. Previously purchased lines remain yours. No future revenue is guaranteed.
